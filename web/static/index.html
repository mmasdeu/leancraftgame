DefGame a Lean game about definitions, not proofs

Typing symbols

Lean uses mathematical symbols that are not on your keyboard. Type a backslash \, then the name, as in VS Code: \forall gives ∀. Some symbols appear by themselves; for the others (like \le or \to, which could be the start of a longer name) press Space or Tab.

Click a row to insert the symbol into your code. Most symbols also have a plain-keyboard spelling that Lean understands.
